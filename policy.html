Policies

Terms, refunds, privacy and our authorship and research ethics policy.

Terms and Conditions

Content on this site is informational. CRF offers research support, webinars, internships and related education. Users must give accurate registration details and may not copy or use content without authorisation. CRF may change or end services at any time.

Refund Policy

Donations and completed services are generally non-refundable. Refunds are considered only for double payments or technical errors, must be requested by email within 7 days of payment, and are processed within 7 to 10 business days.

Privacy Policy

We collect contact and payment details, use them for internal communication and service delivery, and do not share them with third parties other than trusted service providers. You may ask us to correct or delete your data. Questions: admin@consilienceresearch.in.

Authorship and Research Ethics Policy

Version 1.0, effective 1 July 2025. Applies to everyone affiliated with CRF.

  • Authorship: students who independently carry out most of the work may be first authors, with mentors as corresponding authors. Students contributing at least 80% of core tasks may be co-authors. Minor help earns an acknowledgment.
  • AI use: AI tools must be declared and cannot replace analysis or core writing. Authors verify and take responsibility for AI-assisted content.
  • Diligence: keep records, use verifiable data and reproducible methods, and document contribution percentages.
  • Plagiarism: zero tolerance. Manuscripts are checked and must show under 15% similarity, excluding citations. Self-plagiarism must be declared.
  • Data and code: cite sources with access dates and licences, document permission for proprietary data, and credit reused open-source code.
  • Misconduct: concerns go to the CRF Ethics Committee. Penalties can include suspension from authorship, retraction and notifying institutions.
  • Consent: all authors approve the final manuscript. Names change only with written email consent, and CRF keeps an Authorship Contribution Log.
