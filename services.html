What we offer

Services

Sixteen ways we help governments, institutions, industry and communities turn evidence into action.

🎤

Specialized Webinars & Expert Lectures

Expert-led, interactive sessions on sustainability, environmental science, policy and interdisciplinary research, with recordings on demand.

Who benefits: Academics, industry, government, NGOs
🎓

Stakeholder Training & Capacity Building

Customised modules and workshops on environmental management, policy implementation and sustainable development, including ESG compliance and community waste management.

Who benefits: Public sector officials, corporate teams, community leaders
🧐

Technical Review of Proposals & Reports

Evaluation of grant proposals, project reports and policy documents for scientific rigour, methodology and clarity, with detailed feedback.

Who benefits: Researchers, institutions, NGOs, corporates
🌿

Environmental Consulting & Compliance Support

Environmental impact assessments, compliance audits, regulatory guidance (CPCB, MoEFCC) and sustainable operations planning.

Who benefits: Industries, infrastructure projects, public sector
📊

Policy Research & Strategic Insights

Data-driven analysis of policy frameworks, socio-economic trends and environmental challenges, with stakeholder engagement.

Who benefits: Policymakers, think tanks
🤝

Academic-Industry Collaborative Research

Partnership facilitation between universities, research institutions and industry, from project design to funding coordination and implementation.

Who benefits: Universities, institutes, industries
🏛️

Institutional Knowledge Partnerships

Long-term knowledge exchange with academic, government and NGO partners, with shared research frameworks and training resources.

Who benefits: Institutions building research capacity
🧑‍🔬

Internship Program Design & Mentorship

Structured internship programmes and expert mentorship in research, policy analysis, data analysis and project management.

Who benefits: Organisations, universities, students
📈

Impact Assessment & Monitoring Systems

Frameworks and monitoring tools to measure project and policy outcomes using quantitative and qualitative methods.

Who benefits: NGOs, government agencies, corporates
📚

Content & Knowledge Resource Development

White papers, case studies, training manuals and policy briefs, with multilingual options.

Who benefits: Organisations, academia, policymakers
🗺️

Field Research & Baseline Surveys

Survey design and fieldwork to set benchmarks and identify trends in environment, public health and socio-economic areas.

Who benefits: Organisations and governments needing baseline data
🖥️

Data Dashboards & Analytical Reporting

Custom interactive dashboards with real-time metrics, analytical reports and integration with existing data systems.

Who benefits: Corporates, governments, NGOs
✍️

Policy Drafting & Grant Advisory

Drafting of policy documents, guidance on funding opportunities and proposal development aligned with funder requirements.

Who benefits: Governments, NGOs, institutions
♻️

Sustainability & ESG Reporting Solutions

ESG and sustainability reports aligned with GRI, TCFD and SASB, including data collection and analysis.

Who benefits: Corporates, SMEs, institutions
🚛

Integrated Solid Waste Management Planning

Plans covering segregation, recycling, treatment and disposal for urban and rural settings, with a circular economy and community focus.

Who benefits: Municipalities, industries, communities
⛏️

Legacy Waste Remediation & Biomining Advisory

Site assessments, remediation planning and biomining strategies for landfills and dumpsites, focused on resource recovery.

Who benefits: Municipalities, waste agencies
